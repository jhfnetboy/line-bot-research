# Preferences

Owner: jhfnetboy (Chiang Mai, Asia/Bangkok UTC+7). Building EDUAI, Agent24 (edge AI and deployment) and Cos72 (enterprise growth marketing).

## Scope
- LINE Messaging API, LIFF, LINE MINI App, LINE Login, LINE Official Account (OA) features, API changelogs and deprecations.
- LY Corporation and LINE developer news, LINE Developers site notices, LY Tech Blog.
- SDK releases: line/line-bot-sdk-* repos, line-bot-mcp-server, line-openapi, @line/liff, liff-cli.
- LINE platform pricing and policy changes (OA packages, Reply API, message quotas, terms) in Thailand, Japan and Taiwan.
- AI chatbots on LINE: Dify, n8n, Botpress, Typebot, LLM integrations, MCP, agent platforms inside LINE OA.
- Notable LINE bot products and case studies, especially government, healthcare, education, retail and SME use.
- LINE market news in Thailand (first), Japan and Taiwan.

## Style
- English, plain words, no em dashes. Proper nouns as written by the source (Thai and Japanese names kept or romanized as the source does).
- Every item has a real source URL and its date. Never invent data; if a number is not in the source, leave it out.
- Prioritize items that change what a LINE bot developer or OA operator must do (breaking changes, pricing, security) over marketing news.
- 8 to 15 items on a normal day; fewer is fine on quiet days. Say so when nothing changed (for example, no SDK releases).
- Check dates: news search often resurfaces old stories (for example a 2020 press release). Drop anything older than the window unless it is labeled context.

## Blocked / low priority
- LINE TODAY news articles that only mention LINE as the publisher.
- Sticker, music and game promotions unless they involve bots, MINI Apps or AI.
