## Security: LINE OA integration tools (Japan)

### Dentsu Digital's TONARIWA breach leaked LINE OA access tokens; doda's LINE OA is paused

Dentsu Digital said that on Nov 10 it found an attacker had used a leaked credential for a TONARIWA admin account and escalated privileges. Data that may have leaked includes end users' names, birth dates and contact details for some client companies, plus TONARIWA admin data such as access tokens for LINE OA integration, so unauthorized message sending could not be ruled out. Its second report (Nov 12) confirmed leaks at 2 client companies, with counts still being identified, and said the investigation, root-cause work and fixes could take several months in some cases. Persol Career has paused the doda LINE OA. ScanNetSecurity reported the case again on Nov 24. Takeaway for OA operators: if a third-party tool holds your channel access token, know how to revoke and reissue it yourself.

## AI bots on LINE (Japan)

### Do&Do. adds a no-code LINE OA AI bot for gyms to its 'Airtre.gym' service

The new 'Airtre Builder' lets a gym upload PDF, Word, text or Markdown files (up to 20MB each) and use them as the knowledge base for an app agent, a 24-hour LINE OA store guide bot and a staff manual RAG search. To connect LINE, the operator enters the Channel Secret and Channel Access Token and sets the issued webhook URL in LINE Developers; replies use the Reply API with signature verification, and the company says credentials and chats are encrypted.

- Source: https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000018.000123517.html (2026-11-24)

### Fourglobe and Accrete launch 'pimo Gym': LINE OA, LINE MINI App and an AI chatbot on top of existing gym systems

pimo Gym connects to a gym's existing booking system or CRM and runs booking, reminders, follow-ups and an AI chatbot for questions and pre-booking hearing through LINE OA and a LINE MINI App, with LINE notification messages for booking confirmations and SMS or RCS from Accrete as fallback channels. Service started on Oct 15; the first user is Fun up Hamaoka, a mitt-training gym in Omaezaki, Shizuoka. Fourglobe is a LY Corporation Technology Partner in the MINI App and communication categories.

- Source: https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000061.000060555.html (2026-11-20)
- Fourglobe news: https://www.fourglobe.co.jp/news/15715/ (2026-11-20)

## Thailand

### LINE Thailand and Finance Ministry SME program: new coverage says 300,000 SMEs, official text says about 300,000 content views

Follow-up to the Nov 20 item. Bangkok Biz News (Nov 24) and Biztalk News (Nov 23) report the program as aiming to reach 300,000 SMEs, and quote the LINE Thailand CEO on pairing tools with know-how. The Royal Thai Government release says the target is 5,000 on-site trainees a year and about 300,000 online content views a year. Bangkok Biz News also says both sides will set the detailed plan in a next step, so the program is not final yet. Anti-fraud work ties into the Deputy PM's 'Connect the Dots' policy; disaster tools named are LINE ALERT, LINE Safety Check and LIFELINE.

## OA case study (Japan, education)

### Gunma school news site moves its LINE OA from carousel messages to a weekly 'LINE magazine' page

Minna no Gakko Shimbun has sent news as carousel messages on its LINE OA, but the number of articles per message was limited. From Nov 26 it sends a weekly magazine that opens a dedicated page from LINE, with a timeline of the past week's school news plus quizzes, horoscopes and Gunma infectious disease updates. Readers only need to add the OA as a friend; no app install or personal data is required.

- Source: https://np-schools.com/news/29919/ (2026-11-24)
