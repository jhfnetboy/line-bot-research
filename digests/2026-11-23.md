# LINE Bot Research Daily: 2026-11-23

Coverage: 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-23 (Asia/Bangkok, UTC+7). First issue, so it looks back one week, with a short catch-up of LINE Developers notices from Nov 9 to 13 and the LINE Taiwan CONVERGE report.

**Summary:** A breach at COMSBI, a third-party LINE integration tool, exposed LINE OA friend data at 16 Japanese companies, including 1.37 million Nissin friends. Keywords: COMSBI breach, Flex nesting limit, LINE x Thai SMEs, OA Team Management, LINE MINI App + Gemini

## Top story: LINE OA friend data leaked through a third-party SaaS (Japan)

### COMSBI breach: one support account exposed LINE OA friend data of 16 companies

Sonicmoov said an attacker used stolen credentials for its own internal support accounts to log in to COMSBI (SaaS edition), a cloud LINE integration tool, from 23:04 on Nov 9 to the morning of Nov 10 JST, and pulled data for multiple client companies plus cloud config and credentials. Leaked fields include LINE user IDs, display names, profile images and status messages of OA friends, and for some clients names, phone numbers, emails and addresses. Fixes promised: mandatory 2FA on the admin console, IP restrictions, rate limits on bulk reads, and rotation of the LINE OA integration credentials.

- Sonicmoov notice: https://sonicmoov.com/news/5ttj9ndzh (2026-11-12)
- CyberWatch.jp incident page (tally, updated 2026-11-20): https://cyberwatch.jp/incidents/2026-1042/ (2026-11-20)

### Nissin Foods: 1,374,157 LINE OA friends affected, including people who never entered the campaign

Nissin's agency TOPPAN ran a LINE campaign on COMSBI. The tool required importing the whole OA friend list before the draw, so friends who never entered (and some who had blocked the account) were exposed; 543 winners also lost name, address and phone. Nissin says its own OA channel credentials were not taken. The design lesson: do not sync the full follower list into a campaign tool you do not control.

- Source: https://www.nissin.com/jp/company/news/13997/ (2026-11-16)

### Heiwado: up to 328,976 friends of its shop OAs affected

Retailer Heiwado used COMSBI directly for the CoCoRo Plus, kids fest and GOODS DEPO accounts. Leaked: LINE display name, profile image, LINE user ID, gender, birth year and month, and postcode to city level. By Nov 20, CyberWatch.jp counted 16 affected organizations, 761,511 people and 1,458,608 records in their public notices.

- Heiwado notice: https://www.heiwado.jp/news/2026/1117_shop.html (2026-11-17)
- CyberWatch.jp tally: https://cyberwatch.jp/incidents/2026-1042/ (2026-11-20)

## LINE Platform and API changes

### Messaging API: Flex Message box nesting is now capped at 20 levels

Since Nov 19, a Flex Message whose boxes nest deeper than 20 levels (the outermost box is level 1) gets 400 Bad Request. Before this there was no limit. Bots that generate Flex layouts recursively, for example from LLM output or templates, should check their depth.

- Source: https://developers.line.biz/en/news/2026/11/19/flex-message-nested-boxes-validation/ (2026-11-19)

### LY Corporation ends official support for LINE Developer Community in Japan

Official support ends at the end of 2026. The community, started in February 2019, continues from 2027 as the volunteer-run L-Developer Community on connpass and Discord. Its founder Kazumasa Yamamoto said that official docs and AI now make the APIs easier to use, so what to build matters more.

- Source: https://developers.line.biz/en/news/2026/11/18/line-developer-community-support-in-japan-will-end/ (2026-11-18)

## Thailand

### LINE and Thailand's Finance Ministry announce SME AI training, anti-fraud and disaster alert plans

Deputy PM and Finance Minister Ekniti Nitithanprapas visited LY Corporation in Tokyo on Nov 19. Plan one is a one-year SME program with at least 10 practical courses taught by LINE Certified Coaches (LINE OA, content, CRM, targeted ads, AI for planning, campaign analysis and customer replies), aiming for 5,000 on-site trainees a year and about 300,000 online views. LINE adds 22,000 benefits worth about 10.5 million baht (LINE POINTS, LINE OA chat package discounts, LINE Ads credits). The other two plans cover scam prevention and disaster alerts. PRD adds that BOI will look at task-specific AI agent tools for SMEs.

- Royal Thai Government news: https://www.thaigov.go.th/th/news/171478 (2026-11-20)
- Nation Thailand (English): https://www.nationthailand.com/business/tech/40073714 (2026-11-20)
- PRD: BOI to consider AI agent tools for SMEs: https://prd.go.th/main/article/7692536f-1abb-4fc5-b777-b55ea01a2ff6 (2026-11-20)

### FLAIR BKK'26: 45% of Thai luxury buyers buy through LINE chat; LINE previews OA Team Management

LINE Thailand survey figures: 45% buy luxury goods through LINE chat, over 94% of luxury-brand OA followers say OA messages raise purchase intent, and average friend counts of luxury OAs grew 19%. Shoppers accept AI chatbots for routine questions but want human staff for advice. LINE plans an OA Team Management feature where sales staff chat with customers from the brand OA, with assignment, saved chat history and a dashboard, so the relationship stays with the brand when staff change.

- Manager Online: https://mgronline.com/business/detail/9690000110074 (2026-11-17)
- TechMX (OA Team Management detail): https://techmx.net/line-reveals-insights-into-thai-luxury-consumers-ready-to-shop-and-open-to-new-brands/ (2026-11-18)

### Khon Kaen University blood bank reaches 96.4% of donors with LINE OA plus telemedicine

KKU's blood center used LINE OA messages and appointments, then video or voice consults with two-step identity checks, and fell back to phone and letters. It confirmed contact with 162 of 168 donors who needed follow-up. A small but concrete Thai healthcare case for LINE OA workflows.

- Khon Kaen Link: https://www.khonkaenlink.info/read/912519/ (2026-11-17)

## Japan

### Fukuoka City launches an AI waste-sorting LINE MINI App on Nov 24

LY Communications and Fukuoka City launch Gomi Shirube: take a photo or type an item name, and generative AI (Gemini API) gives the city's sorting category and disposal tips, in 11 languages, with no install or signup. It replaces the city OA's keyword search, which got over 30,000 lookups a month in 2024. It will also be reachable from the @fukuokacity OA from about Jan 15, 2027. In an Oct 2026 survey of 78 users, 98.7% said they would keep using it.

- Source: https://lycomm.co.jp/ja/press/2026/111915 (2026-11-19)

### LY Corporation's Agent i comes to LINE MUSIC as AI PlayList

Agent i, LY's merged AI agent brand (formerly Yahoo! JAPAN AI Assistant and LINE AI), now builds and suggests playlists inside LINE MUSIC 6.39.0+, with LINE Login required. LY says the feature uses Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform or the OpenAI API, and will reach Agent i in the LINE app and Yahoo! JAPAN app later.

- Source: https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001851.000129774.html (2026-11-17)

### LINE client team runs coding agents in the cloud: 375 to about 12,000 tasks a month in four months

LY engineer onevcat describes Agent Cluster, an in-house platform that runs coding agents in the cloud under LY's security rules, with no tokens handed to agents and CI reworked for agent-paced verification. Monthly tasks grew from 375 to nearly 12,000 while total cost grew 7.5 times.

- Source: https://techblog.lycorp.co.jp/ja/20261116b (2026-11-16)

## Catch-up: notices from Nov 9 to 13 (first issue only)

### API Playground in the Messaging API reference shuts down on Nov 30

LINE is removing the in-browser API Playground after reviewing usage and security requirements; the API itself is unaffected. Use curl with the request examples in the reference instead.

- Source: https://developers.line.biz/en/news/2026/11/13/api-playground-discontinuation/ (2026-11-13)

### New doc: how to stop displaying rich menus

A new page covers stopping default and per-user rich menus and rich menu aliases. Deleting a rich menu without unlinking it leaves it on screen until the user re-enters the chat.

- Source: https://developers.line.biz/en/news/2026/11/13/rich-menu/ (2026-11-13)

### Verified MINI App search results in Japan now show category, description and service badges

From LINE 26.18.0, search results in Japan show category, description and up to two badges (such as Mobile Order or Membership), generated from the channel description. Write a clear channel description.

- Source: https://developers.line.biz/en/news/2026/11/10/mini-app-search/ (2026-11-10)

### LIFF v2.32.0 brings back switching the OA shown on the MINI App consent screen

LIFF v2.32.0 restores the option, paused on Sep 15, 2026, to choose which LINE Official Account appears on the channel consent screen for the add friend option in LINE MINI Apps.

- Source: https://developers.line.biz/en/news/2026/11/09/release-liff-2-32-0/ (2026-11-09)

### LINE Taiwan targets Q1 2027 for an AI Agent Platform inside LINE Official Accounts

LY's report on LINE CONVERGE 2026 says Taiwan has over 3.6 million LINE Official Accounts (as of Aug 2026) and 22 million monthly users. LINE Taiwan plans an AI Agent Platform that lets businesses build their own agents inside their OA, starting in Q1 2027. iThome reported on Oct 13 that the platform is no-code, starts with booking, sales and customer service agents, and will support MCP.
